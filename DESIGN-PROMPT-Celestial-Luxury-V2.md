# 夢境圖書館・Celestial Luxury V2 視覺提示詞

## 主提示詞
A lavish fantasy mobile UI for a “Dream Library,” blending deep indigo, violet, and midnight blue with warm champagne-gold accents. A moonlit mystical library filled with stars, candles, glowing books, crystal orbs and soft magical dust. Premium glassmorphism cards with layered translucency, subtle inner highlights, rounded 22–30px corners, luminous violet borders, cinematic depth and soft bloom. Elegant Chinese serif typography paired with a refined English serif title. The overall feeling is dreamy, mysterious, intimate, luxurious and collectible — like a high-end fantasy mobile game, but still clean, readable and usable.

Hero section: an illustrated dream librarian named Luna in a grand celestial library, framed by moonlight and candlelight, with profile level, soul sync, daily conversation and library level displayed as floating glass cards. Use clear foreground/background separation, strong focal hierarchy and restrained glow.

Primary CTA: large purple-violet gradient card with gold edge light, dream icon, strong headline and soft subtitle.

Emotion row: six compact rounded-square dream emotion icons, each with its own soft illustrated atmosphere rather than flat emoji-only treatment.

Feature section: two-column cards. Each card combines one large Chinese character icon, title, subtitle and a small thematic miniature scene (dream portal, library shelves, glowing heart, treasure chest, crystal sphere, soul silhouettes, lotus, vinyl/music). Use alternating violet and plum tones, with subtle warm highlights.

Recent dreams: horizontal compact story cards with thumbnail artwork, title, date and tags.

Bottom navigation: celestial floating dock, dark translucent background, gold/violet line details, a larger central Luna/moon orb, soft cloud or nebula ambience.

## 色彩
- Background: #080516 / #0A0618 / #110824
- Deep violet: #3B1C71 / #5B2EB5
- Accent violet: #7A3CFF / #A45CFF
- Champagne gold: #F2C46F / #FFE4A8
- Primary text: #F8F2FF
- Secondary text: #A996C8
- Glass line: rgba(204,164,255,.24)

## 材質
- Glassmorphism with 14–22px blur
- 1px translucent violet borders
- Soft internal highlight
- Large 22–30px corner radius
- Deep soft shadow, no harsh black edge
- Subtle star particles and bloom
- Warm candle highlights mixed with cool moonlight

## 網頁落地原則
1. 不把整個頁面做成一張大圖；所有按鈕、文字、卡片與導航維持真實 HTML。
2. Hero 人物可使用獨立插畫，但狀態卡、CTA、功能入口必須維持可點擊。
3. 手機優先，390–430px 寬度先調整。
4. 功能卡固定兩欄，不再用四欄小卡造成擁擠。
5. 字體層級清楚：品牌 30–34px、主要 CTA 20–24px、卡片標題 17–20px、說明 11–13px。
6. 光效只用於主視覺、CTA 與重點卡；不要每個元件都發光。
7. 任何新產生的 UI 示意圖，都要同步保存：色票、漸層、材質、圓角、陰影、字體層級、間距、背景圖像提示詞與可落地 CSS 規則。
